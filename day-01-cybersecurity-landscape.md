# Day 01-The Cybersecurity Landscape

**Programme:** MyFirstHack — 90 Days  
**Topic:** Understanding the Digital World  
**Status:** Completed

## What I learned

### 1. Cybersecurity Fundamentals
- Meaning and purpose of cybersecurity.
- Protecting digital systems, networks, data and people from cyber threats.
- Why cybersecurity matters in everyday life.

### 2. Real-World Cyberattack
**Case study: Colonial Pipeline (2021)**

Learned how a ransomware incident affected critical infrastructure and disrupted fuel operations.

### 3. Cybersecurity Career Paths

**SOC Analyst — Security Operations Center**
- Monitoring security alerts.
- Investigating logs.
- Identifying suspicious activities.
- Understanding SIEM (Security Information and Event Management).

**GRC Analyst — Governance, Risk and Compliance**
- Security policies.
- Risk assessments.
- Audits.
- Regulatory compliance.

### 4. Practical Exploration
**Tool:** Have I Been Pwned

- Checked whether my email address appeared in known data breaches.
- Learned how exposed email addresses can indicate potential account-security risks.

## Key Takeaway

Cybersecurity is much broader than hacking. It involves technology, investigation, threat detection, risk management and people.

## Reflection

Day 1 helped me understand the cybersecurity landscape and introduced me to two different career paths: SOC and GRC.
